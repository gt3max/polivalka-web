Privacy Policy

PlantApp · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data PlantApp ("we", "us", the "App") collects, how we use it, and your choices. The App is operated by the PlantApp team. Questions: support@plantapp.pro.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data — your email address and a securely hashed password. Optional display name.
  • Content you create — plants you save, watering/care history, journal notes, and onboarding answers.
  • Photos you submit — images you take or pick to identify a plant or to ask the AI Doctor about a problem. A photo you send to the AI Doctor is stored with that consultation so you can see it again later, and is deleted together with it. Before a photo leaves your device, the App removes its metadata, such as the location where it was taken.
  • Location — only if you grant permission, used to tailor seasonal watering and outdoor advice. The App rounds your coordinates to about 1 km before sending or storing them. On your account we keep these rounded coordinates, your city and country, your climate zone and average monthly temperatures. You can disable location at any time.
  • Basic technical data — app version, build number and device type needed to run the service. If the App crashed, it sends a short crash report with its next request: the type of error, a code identifying it and the build number, without any content of your data; it appears only in our server logs.
  • IP address — used to protect the service from abuse (limits on sign-ups and on requests made without an account) and kept for up to 2 days.
  • Subscription status — if you buy PlantApp Premium, Apple gives us a receipt that we check to confirm which plan is active and when it expires. We never see your card, billing address or Apple Account details: payment is handled entirely by Apple.
  • Optional improvement data — off by default. If you turn on "Help improve Identifier", we keep anonymous outcomes (e.g. a photo hash and which services agreed). If you turn on "Help improve Doctor", we also keep the text of your question as you typed it (up to 2,000 characters), a photo hash and your plan type — without your email address or account. These records are not linked to you, are kept indefinitely to improve accuracy, and are not deleted when you delete your account, so please do not include personal details in your questions while this option is on.

2. How we use data

  • To identify plants, provide care information, and run AI Doctor diagnosis.
  • To save your plants and send local watering reminders.
  • To create and secure your account.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. Services that receive your photos and queries

The App asks for your permission before it sends a photo for the first time. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy; without it, photos are not sent.

To identify plants and diagnose problems, the photo or query you submit is sent to third-party plant-recognition and AI services that return the result: iNaturalist, Pl@ntNet, Google (Gemini), Cloudflare, Groq and OpenRouter (which passes the request to the model provider it routes to: Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and DeepSeek models served only from the US or EU, never from China). For an image search we use SerpApi: the photo is made available to Google through a temporary link for Google Lens, and SerpApi keeps the search data for 31 days. Each of these providers handles the data under its own terms; some keep submitted data for a period and may use it to improve their services. Plant images are delivered via image CDNs (Cloudinary, ImageKit), and the service is hosted on Amazon Web Services, which also sends our verification emails.

The App also contacts some services directly from your device: Apple, to turn your rounded coordinates into a city name; Open-Meteo (OpenMeteo GmbH, Switzerland), for weather and climate data for your rounded coordinates — it may keep server logs, which can include coordinates, for up to 90 days; and GitHub, to download updates of the plant identifier that works on your device — GitHub sees your IP address.

Plant.id (Kindwise, FlowerChecker s.r.o., Czech Republic) is used only if you separately allow it — as a last-resort check when the other services disagree, and for the plant health check in the AI Doctor. It is off unless you turn it on. Under Kindwise's terms, a photo sent to Plant.id may be kept by Kindwise (its copies of users' data are anonymised after 6 months), used to develop its software and AI, shown to its other customers, and published under the Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0 licence, with faces blurred. A photo that has been published or used for training cannot be withdrawn later, even if you turn the permission off. Please do not photograph people, documents or anything private. Plant.id receives only the image — not your email, account or location.

4. Legal bases and international transfers (EEA and UK)

We send your photo or query to the services above to provide the result you asked for (performance of our contract with you). We send photos to Plant.id only on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the App; withdrawal does not affect photos already sent. Where a provider is outside the EEA or the UK (SerpApi, Google, Cloudflare, Groq, OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek's US/EU hosts, Apple, GitHub and Amazon Web Services), we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum in that provider's data-processing terms, or on an adequacy decision where the provider is certified under it (GitHub, for example, is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension). Kindwise is located in the EU. Open-Meteo is located in Switzerland, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection.

5. Data retention & deletion

We keep your saved plants, care history and journal notes while your account exists. A plant you delete from My Plants is erased at once, together with its care history.

AI Doctor consultations are kept for a limited time and then deleted automatically: 30 days on the free tier and 12 months on Premium, together with the text of your questions and the answers. A consultation you delete yourself stays recoverable for 30 days and is then erased for good, together with any photo attached to it.

You can delete your account and associated data at any time in the App: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent.

To stop the free limits from being reset by deleting and re-creating an account, we keep one item after deletion until the end of the current calendar month: a one-way fingerprint of your email address (a hash, from which the address cannot be recovered) and how many free identifications and AI Doctor consultations were used that month; it is erased automatically when the month ends. We also keep the cost records of the paid AI calls made for the account, under the same fingerprint and without your address, because they are part of our accounting.

Beyond this, your data remains only in: (1) backups of our user database, kept for up to 60 days and then overwritten automatically — data you delete disappears from them within that period; (2) server logs, in which your email address is masked, kept for 30 days; (3) the anonymous improvement records described in section 1, if you turned that option on. Copies already held by the third-party services named in section 3 are governed by their terms.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and the UK. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data via the App, or by contacting us. You can withdraw your permission to send photos, and your consent for Plant.id, at any time in Settings → Privacy. Depending on your region (e.g. EEA/UK/California) you may have additional rights, including the right to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; contact us to exercise them.

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
